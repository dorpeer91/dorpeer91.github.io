## About the project

The series takes place in an unnamed industrial town, haunted by a plague that causes people who get sick to develop terrifying mutations. The source of the plague is unknown, and while there are some similarities between cases, each manifestation is unique. 

Throughout the series we follow David, a clerk assigned to visit different people who are infected by the disease and report back. As he visits more and more patients he tries to find different and subtle ways to help them, while learning more about the plague along the way.

I'm currently working on the first episode of the series, writing, storyboarding and animating it myself. I'm hoping to be able to produce a few episodes independently, but at the moment I am mainly focused on producing first one.
