I am a filmmaker and visual artist whose work spans film, video, animation, motion design and illustration. 

I work for many years as a freelance motion designer in the corporate sector, while developing my own personal projects on my spare time. While my professional freelance work is very sleek and clean, my personal art explores darker themes and more hidden aspects of the psyche.

My short films have screened at notable festivals including Hollyshorts, Filmquest and the Austin Arthouse Film Festivals.

I am currently working on a short animated film called Shuttered.
