I am a filmmaker and visual artist. My work spans film, video, animation, motion design and illustration.

I've worked for many years as a freelance motion designer in the corporate sector, while developing my own personal projects in my spare time. While my professional freelance work is very sleek and clean, my personal art explores the subconscious mind, and the negative and repressed aspects of both the personal psyche and the stuff we repress collectively, as a society. 

I find that often the unintentional in art (Such as in automatic drawing) can be reflective of the parts of the mind that unconsciously kept hidden, therefore art has served me greatly as a form of therapy, but I think whatever is reflected in an artist's work from their own personal life can also be reflective of something in the life of a stranger looking at / experiencing the art as well.

My short films have screened at notable festivals including Hollyshorts, Filmquest and the Austin Arthouse Film Festivals.

I currenty live and work in Berlin.
