I’ve been recently getting the feeling, that we’re all (Humanity) sandwiched between the past and the future, with **no present in sight**. What does that mean? 

In many facet of art and we seem to be in a growing obsession with **Nostalgia**. I started noticing it almost 20 years ago, when indie bands I was listening to as a teenager (Like the Black Keys, MGMT and Unknown Mortal Orchestra) seemed to try and sound like they were old bands from the 60's or 70's. There was a known upsurge of the Hipster culture, i.e. listening to vinyl records and appreciating older techniques and styles of recording, producing etc. 
But this is not just music, you see it all across culture. It usually starts in the fringes and the indie scene. You started seeing Video games who borrow 16bit bit aesthetic, and movies shot on actual film. I thought that this will be a trend that will soon pass, but as the years passed, the contemporary culture has kept looking backward for inspiration. It seemed that somewhere around the early 2000's culture has struck some sort of panic of the future, and tried to throw an anchor back in time into the 60's, but as it turns out the anchor fails to stay put, the anchor of nostalgia just kept moving with the years since. We've seen a surge of 80's nostalgia, 90's nostalgia, and now we're finally entering early 2000's nostalgia, with the surge of old web personal websites and video games imitating the aesthetic of PS2 era video games. At this pace at some point we will have to be nostalgic about past nostalgia. 

What seems to be a slightly annoying fad started in recent years looking like a collective refusal to grow up, and AI has sort of became this vague answer to "What were we so afraid off". Basically what may have happened is that the internet in a weird way became more of a summarizer of human culture, and then AI allows us to forever bathe in this catalogue of our entire human existence.

I came up with a thought that might be a cursor of sort-of, maybe, getting out of this suffocating burrito. As we are squeezed between the past and the future, perhaps there needs to be a “new” way of moving forward. My idea is to “move into the present”. I'll explain: Artists usually go towards what feels new / different, sort of like going into the future, but AI has proved that the future eats itself like an ouroboros and merges with the past. THEREFORE - the better way to move forward as a culture and as artists is to move into the present. Because all this nostalgia might also just be the byproduct of a culture that is too much in talk with itself, where everything is a homage to a reference to a reference. But the only thing that is truly infinitely original is the present. It’s only when you start drawing people from observation you see more and more nuances. 

The only artist I can think of right now that actually does it is Hong Sang Too. If you watch one of his movies you’ll see what I mean. It really looks like he was looking at how reality is right now and how people are and then just tried capturing it in a movie. And the last movie I saw of his “What Does that Nature Says to You” uses the shittiest HD DV camera that give it such a non-elevated day to day aesehetic that it really does feel like a slice of life, making it ever more fresh (To me, at least)

OK. I don’t know, I just wanted to get that thought out there; in short I would say: In a time where we are crushed between the past and the future, we should go into the present.
